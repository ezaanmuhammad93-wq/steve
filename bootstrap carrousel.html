TOP 3 anime YOU should watch!

One Piece

One Piece is an anime about a group of pirates who are searching for the ultimate treasure, the One Piece, in order to become the Pirate King.

Hell's paradise

Hell's paradise is an anime about a group of students who are sent to a school for the elite, where they must compete for the ultimate prize.

Vinland saga

Vinland Saga is an anime about a young warrior named Thorfinn who seeks revenge against the man who killed his father. The story follows his journey through a world of violence and political intrigue.
